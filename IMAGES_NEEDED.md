## Where to Find Free Images

### Recommended Sources (Free & No Attribution Required):
1. **Unsplash** - https://unsplash.com/
   - Search: "coding", "programming", "developer"
   
2. **Pexels** - https://www.pexels.com/
   - Search: "technology", "coding", "computer"
   
3. **Pixabay** - https://pixabay.com/
   - Search: "programming", "algorithm", "data"

4. **unDraw** - https://undraw.co/
   - Free SVG illustrations (programming themed)

5. **Freepik** - https://www.freepik.com/
   - Free resources with attribution

## Image Format Recommendations
- **JPG**: For photographs and complex images
- **PNG**: For images with transparency or simple graphics
- **WebP**: For better compression (modern format)
