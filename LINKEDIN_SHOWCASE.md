## 🎥 **Recording Tips**

### **Technical Setup:**
- Use **1920x1080** resolution
- Record at **30 FPS** minimum
- Enable **system audio** for click sounds
- Use **Chrome/Edge** for best compatibility

### **Recording Best Practices:**
1. **Clear browser cache** before recording
2. **Close unnecessary tabs** and applications
3. **Move mouse slowly** to show interactions
4. **Pause briefly** after each action
5. **Keep cursor visible** throughout demo

### **Visual Tips:**
- Use **full-screen browser** (F11)
- **Zoom to 100%** for clarity
- **Smooth transitions** between pages
- **Highlight key features** with cursor
